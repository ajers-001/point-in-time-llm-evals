## Known pitfalls we must answer

Paleka, Goel, Geiping and Tramèr (2025), "Pitfalls in Evaluating Language Model Forecasters", arXiv 2506.00723
(ICLR 2026):

- **Questions chosen with hindsight leak the answer.** Ours were written by the forecasting sites at the time and
  picked by a fixed rule. One residue remains: "30 days before close" depends on when a question closed, and some
  close early when the event happens.
- **Text dated wrongly.** This is the background-note problem we found (30 of 412); the two filters address it.

## Related benchmark to position against

Benhenda (2026), "Look-Ahead-Bench: a Standardized Benchmark of Look-ahead Bias in Point-in-Time LLMs for
Finance", arXiv 2601.13770. It measures look-ahead bias in point-in-time models through trading results (alpha
decay). Ours measures it on dated yes/no questions against the crowd forecast. Not read beyond the abstract.

## What has no source

- The 25% bar for "clean" (verdict 4). No standard exists that we found.
- The utility test (verdict 5).
- The simple leak score. It is our own plain-number companion and decides nothing.
